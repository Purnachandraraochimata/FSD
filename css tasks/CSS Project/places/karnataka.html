Karnataka

Karnataka features everything from vibrant Bangalore nightlife and royal palaces in Mysore to the relaxed beaches of the Karavali coast and lush Western Ghats.

  • Top attractions: Mysore Palace, Hampi ruins, Coorg coffee plantations, and Bangalore’s tech-and-dine scene.
  • Activities: heritage sightseeing, trekking in the Western Ghats, coffee and spice plantation tours.
  • Travel tips: Hampi is best in cooler months; Coorg is popular during monsoon for its waterfalls.
