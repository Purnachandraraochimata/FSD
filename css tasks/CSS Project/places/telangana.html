Telangana

Telangana blends historic Hyderabad landmarks with a lively food culture and scenic nearby hills, offering city tourism alongside heritage experiences.

  • Top attractions: Charminar, Golconda Fort, Ramoji Film City, and the Hussain Sagar lakefront.
  • Activities: heritage walks, street-food tasting (Hyderabadi biryani), and film-studio tours.
  • Travel tips: Hyderabad is year-round but cooler months (Nov–Feb) are best for sightseeing.
