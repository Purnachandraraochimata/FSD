Kerala

Kerala — "God's Own Country" — is famed for its tranquil backwaters, lush tea and spice plantations, traditional art forms, and restorative Ayurveda retreats.

  • Top attractions: Alleppey backwaters (houseboats), Munnar tea gardens, Fort Kochi heritage, and Periyar wildlife reserve.
  • Activities: houseboat cruises, Ayurvedic spa programs, spice-plantation tours, and birdwatching.
  • Travel tips: monsoon months bring rain and greenery; for houseboats choose Oct–Feb for calm waters.
