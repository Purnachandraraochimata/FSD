Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh is known for its rich temple heritage, long sandy coastlines, and flavorful South Indian cuisine — ideal for culture seekers and beach travelers.

  • Top attractions: Tirupati (Venkateswara Temple), Araku Valley, Vizag (Ramakrishna Beach) and the Borra Caves.
  • Activities: temple pilgrimages, hill-station trekking, coastal dining and coffee plantation visits.
  • Travel tips: best visited Oct–Mar for pleasant weather; local seafood and biryani are must-tries.
