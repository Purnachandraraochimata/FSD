Chennai

Chennai is a coastal metropolis with classical South Indian culture, historic temples, long beaches and an acclaimed food scene focused on traditional Tamil cuisine.

  • Top attractions: Marina Beach, Kapaleeshwarar Temple, Fort St. George, and the Government Museum.
  • Activities: beach walks, temple visits, classical music and dance performances, and sampling local Chettinad and filter coffee.
  • Travel tips: avoid midday heat; evenings on Marina Beach are pleasant and lively.
