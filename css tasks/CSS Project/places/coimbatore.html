Coimbatore

Coimbatore is a gateway city to the Nilgiris with a strong textile heritage, pleasant nearby hill stations and easy access to nature and industry tours.

  • Top attractions: short trips to Ooty and Coonoor, Marudamalai temple, and local textile bazaars.
  • Activities: hill-station excursions, local food tasting, and heritage-market shopping.
  • Travel tips: ideal as a base for Nilgiri hills; plan trips early to avoid traffic on hill roads.
